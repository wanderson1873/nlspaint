Exterior House Painting · Cumberland

What's included

Local, not a franchise. Most exteriors we paint in Cumberland are wood clapboard colonials and capes on Cumberland Hill and around Arnold Mills, plus vinyl-sided raised ranches near Diamond Hill. The wood homes need the most prep: years of sun on the south side leave peeling paint that has to be scraped and primed before any color goes on.

Because we're only ten minutes away in Woonsocket, we can move fast when a dry week opens up in the forecast — which matters in a short New England painting season.

That is why every exterior job we take on starts with prep: we power wash the house, scrape and sand every loose spot, caulk the gaps and prime bare wood before a single finish coat goes on. Paint only lasts as long as the surface underneath it.

  • Power washingDirt, chalk and mildew washed off so the new paint bonds to the surface, not to the grime.
  • Scraping & sandingLoose and peeling paint removed, edges feathered smooth so they don't show through.
  • Caulking & glazingGaps around windows, doors and trim sealed against water and drafts.
  • Spot primingBare wood and repairs primed with an exterior primer that blocks stains and tannins.
  • Two finish coatsPremium exterior paint (Benjamin Moore or Sherwin-Williams) brushed, rolled or sprayed.
  • Trim, doors & shuttersCrisp lines on trim, front doors, shutters, railings and garage doors.
  • Property protectionPlants, walkways, windows and fixtures covered and masked before we spray.
  • Clean-up & walkthroughPaint chips picked up every day and a final walkthrough with you before we leave.

Exterior Painting FAQ

Common questions

Still wondering about something? Text us — a real person answers.

Do you charge extra to come to Cumberland?

No. Cumberland is right next door to Woonsocket, and estimates are always free.

How soon can you start a job in Cumberland?

Small jobs often start within a week. Exteriors are scheduled from May to October.

When is the best time to paint a house exterior in Rhode Island?

From May to October. Paint needs dry days above about 50°F, so we schedule around the weather and keep you posted if rain moves the start date.

How long does an exterior paint job last?

With proper prep and two coats of quality paint, siding usually looks good for 7–10 years. Trim and doors facing the sun may need a touch-up sooner.

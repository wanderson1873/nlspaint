Our home town

House painting in Woonsocket, RI

Woonsocket is home base. We paint the triple-deckers and mill-era multifamilies in Social, Fairmount and the Globe, the capes and colonials in the North End and East Woonsocket, and storefronts and commercial buildings across the city.

Triple-deckers and older multifamilies are a specialty: tall walls, lots of porches and trim, and landlords who need units turned over between tenants without losing a month of rent.

Because we're local, we can come out the same week for a free estimate and start small jobs within days.

FAQ

Good questions

Still wondering about something? Text us — a real person answers.

Do you charge for estimates?

No. Estimates are always free — just text us a few photos, or we'll come out for bigger jobs.

Do you paint furnished homes?

Yes. We move and cover your furniture and floors before we start, and put everything back when we're done.

Who buys the paint?

We do. Paint and materials are included in your quote, and you choose the colors. If you already have paint, tell us and we'll quote labor only.

How fast can you start?

Small jobs often start within a week. We'll give you a start date with your quote.

What payment do you accept?

Cash, check, Zelle and Venmo. A small deposit (about 20%) covers materials on bigger jobs.

Do you charge extra to travel to my town?

No. The price is the same in Woonsocket and in every town we serve in Rhode Island and Massachusetts.

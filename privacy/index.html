Privacy Policy

Short and plain: what we collect, why, and what we don't do with it.

What we collect

When you fill out the quote form, we receive your name, phone number, town, the service you need and your message, plus the page you sent it from. When you text or call us, we see your phone number and anything you choose to send, like photos.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and prepare your estimate.
  • To schedule and do the work, and follow up afterwards.

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't add you to marketing lists.

Website analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages lead to a text, a call or a form. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as pages viewed, device type and approximate location. It doesn't tell us who you are. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Form delivery

The quote form is delivered to our email by a form service (Formspree or Web3Forms), which processes the data only to send it to us.

Photos of your property

We only publish photos of a job with your permission. We never publish your street address — at most the town — and we remove location data from every photo before it goes online.

Questions

Text or call (774) 586-9824 and we'll answer, or ask us to delete what we have about you.

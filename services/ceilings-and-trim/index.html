Ceiling & Trim Painting

What's included

Trim is where you can tell a pro from a weekend job. Drips on the baseboards, gaps in the corners and brush marks on doors stand out every time you walk by.

We sand and caulk every joint, use a stain-blocking primer on ceilings and finish trim in a hard, washable semi-gloss. Doors get both sides and the edges. Ceilings get cut in by hand and rolled in one direction so there's no flashing.

Inside and out: baseboards, window and door casings, crown molding, stair railings and spindles, porch railings, steps and decks.

  • CeilingsFlat ceiling paint, stain-blocking primer on water marks, even roller passes.
  • Baseboards & casingsSanded, caulked and finished in a durable semi-gloss.
  • DoorsBoth sides and all edges, brushed or sprayed off the hinges when needed.
  • Crown moldingClean lines where the wall meets the ceiling.
  • Stairs & railingsSpindles, handrails and risers painted, treads stained or painted.
  • Porch steps & railingsExterior trim, porch floors and steps with porch & floor paint.
  • Caulking every jointGaps and nail holes filled so the trim reads as one clean piece.
  • Careful maskingFloors, walls and hardware masked so the lines stay sharp.

Ceilings & Trim FAQ

Common questions

Still wondering about something? Text us — a real person answers.

Can you cover water stains on the ceiling?

Yes, once the leak is fixed. We seal the stain with a stain-blocking primer first so it doesn't come back through the new paint.

Do you paint popcorn ceilings?

Yes, we can roll or spray them. Removing popcorn texture is a separate job that we quote on its own.

Brush or spray for trim and doors?

Both. We brush most trim in occupied homes and spray doors and trim in empty homes or new construction for the smoothest finish.

Can you paint porch steps and railings?

Yes. We scrape, prime and use a porch & floor paint made for foot traffic and weather.
